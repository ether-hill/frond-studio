shown on screen while each model is displayed.

Species without an entry (e.g. Orchid Bee, Bee Hummingbird, Soldier Beetle)
show the procedural build. To add more: drop a `.glb` here and add an entry to
`MODEL_ASSETS` in `components/projects/pollinator-lab/species.ts` keyed by the
species id, with a `credit`.
